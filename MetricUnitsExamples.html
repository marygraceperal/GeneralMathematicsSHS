✏️ Step-by-Step Unit Conversions

Math Study Guide: Worked Examples for Each Unit Type

1. Metric System Conversions

A. Kilometers to Meters (\(\text{km} \rightarrow \text{m}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ km} = 1,000 \text{ m}\)
Example 1: Convert \(3.2 \text{ km}\) to meters.
\(3.2 \text{ km} \times \frac{1,000 \text{ m}}{1 \text{ km}} = \mathbf{3,200 \text{ m}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(0.75 \text{ km}\) to meters.
\(0.75 \text{ km} \times \frac{1,000 \text{ m}}{1 \text{ km}} = \mathbf{750 \text{ m}}\)

B. Meters to Centimeters (\(\text{m} \rightarrow \text{cm}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm}\)
Example 1: Convert \(2.5 \text{ m}\) to centimeters.
\(2.5 \text{ m} \times \frac{100 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ m}} = \mathbf{250 \text{ cm}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(0.18 \text{ m}\) to centimeters.
\(0.18 \text{ m} \times \frac{100 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ m}} = \mathbf{18 \text{ cm}}\)

C. Grams to Milligrams (\(\text{g} \rightarrow \text{mg}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ g} = 1,000 \text{ mg}\)
Example 1: Convert \(5 \text{ g}\) to milligrams.
\(5 \text{ g} \times \frac{1,000 \text{ mg}}{1 \text{ g}} = \mathbf{5,000 \text{ mg}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(0.42 \text{ g}\) to milligrams.
\(0.42 \text{ g} \times \frac{1,000 \text{ mg}}{1 \text{ g}} = \mathbf{420 \text{ mg}}\)

2. US Customary Conversions

A. Feet to Inches (\(\text{ft} \rightarrow \text{in}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ ft} = 12 \text{ in}\)
Example 1: Convert \(6 \text{ ft}\) to inches.
\(6 \text{ ft} \times \frac{12 \text{ in}}{1 \text{ ft}} = \mathbf{72 \text{ in}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(4.5 \text{ ft}\) to inches.
\(4.5 \text{ ft} \times \frac{12 \text{ in}}{1 \text{ ft}} = \mathbf{54 \text{ in}}\)

B. Pounds to Ounces (\(\text{lb} \rightarrow \text{oz}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ lb} = 16 \text{ oz}\)
Example 1: Convert \(3 \text{ lb}\) to ounces.
\(3 \text{ lb} \times \frac{16 \text{ oz}}{1 \text{ lb}} = \mathbf{48 \text{ oz}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(2.25 \text{ lb}\) to ounces.
\(2.25 \text{ lb} \times \frac{16 \text{ oz}}{1 \text{ lb}} = \mathbf{36 \text{ oz}}\)

C. Gallons to Quarts (\(\text{gal} \rightarrow \text{qt}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ gal} = 4 \text{ qt}\)
Example 1: Convert \(4 \text{ gal}\) to quarts.
\(4 \text{ gal} \times \frac{4 \text{ qt}}{1 \text{ gal}} = \mathbf{16 \text{ qt}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(1.5 \text{ gal}\) to quarts.
\(1.5 \text{ gal} \times \frac{4 \text{ qt}}{1 \text{ gal}} = \mathbf{6 \text{ qt}}\)

3. Inter-System Conversions (Customary \(\leftrightarrow\) Metric)

A. Miles to Kilometers (\(\text{mi} \rightarrow \text{km}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ mi} \approx 1.60934 \text{ km}\)
Example 1: Convert \(10 \text{ mi}\) to kilometers.
\(10 \text{ mi} \times 1.60934 = \mathbf{16.0934 \text{ km}}\)
Example 2: Convert a marathon distance of \(26.2 \text{ mi}\) to kilometers.
\(26.2 \text{ mi} \times 1.60934 = \mathbf{42.1647 \text{ km}}\)

B. Kilometers to Miles (\(\text{km} \rightarrow \text{mi}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ km} \approx 0.621371 \text{ mi}\)
Example 1: Convert a \(5 \text{ km}\) run to miles.
\(5 \text{ km} \times 0.621371 = \mathbf{3.10686 \text{ mi}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(100 \text{ km}\) to miles.
\(100 \text{ km} \times 0.621371 = \mathbf{62.1371 \text{ mi}}\)

C. Pounds to Kilograms (\(\text{lb} \rightarrow \text{kg}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ lb} \approx 0.453592 \text{ kg}\)
Example 1: Convert \(150 \text{ lb}\) to kilograms.
\(150 \text{ lb} \times 0.453592 = \mathbf{68.0388 \text{ kg}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(12 \text{ lb}\) to kilograms.
\(12 \text{ lb} \times 0.453592 = \mathbf{5.4431 \text{ kg}}\)

D. Kilograms to Pounds (\(\text{kg} \rightarrow \text{lb}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ kg} \approx 2.20462 \text{ lb}\)
Example 1: Convert \(70 \text{ kg}\) to pounds.
\(70 \text{ kg} \times 2.20462 = \mathbf{154.3234 \text{ lb}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(2.5 \text{ kg}\) to pounds.
\(2.5 \text{ kg} \times 2.20462 = \mathbf{5.51155 \text{ lb}}\)

E. Gallons to Liters (\(\text{gal} \rightarrow \text{L}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ gal} \approx 3.78541 \text{ L}\)
Example 1: Convert \(2 \text{ gal}\) to liters.
\(2 \text{ gal} \times 3.78541 = \mathbf{7.57082 \text{ L}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(12 \text{ gal}\) to liters.
\(12 \text{ gal} \times 3.78541 = \mathbf{45.4249 \text{ L}}\)

F. Liters to Gallons (\(\text{L} \rightarrow \text{gal}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ L} \approx 0.264172 \text{ gal}\)
Example 1: Convert \(10 \text{ L}\) to gallons.
\(10 \text{ L} \times 0.264172 = \mathbf{2.64172 \text{ gal}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(50 \text{ L}\) to gallons.
\(50 \text{ L} \times 0.264172 = \mathbf{13.2086 \text{ gal}}\)

G. Inches to Centimeters (\(\text{in} \rightarrow \text{cm}\))

Conversion Factor: \(1 \text{ in} = 2.54 \text{ cm}\)
Example 1: Convert \(10 \text{ in}\) to centimeters.
\(10 \text{ in} \times 2.54 = \mathbf{25.4 \text{ cm}}\)
Example 2: Convert \(5.5 \text{ in}\) to centimeters.
\(5.5 \text{ in} \times 2.54 = \mathbf{13.97 \text{ cm}}\)
