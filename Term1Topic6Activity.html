📖 Quick Reference: Conversion Factors

Conversion Category Units / Direction Standard Conversion Factor
Metric Length & Mass \(\text{km} \rightarrow \text{m}\), \(\text{m} \rightarrow \text{cm}\), \(\text{g} \rightarrow \text{mg}\) \(1 \text{ km} = 1,000 \text{ m}\), \(1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm}\), \(1 \text{ g} = 1,000 \text{ mg}\)
US Customary Units \(\text{ft} \rightarrow \text{in}\), \(\text{lb} \rightarrow \text{oz}\), \(\text{gal} \rightarrow \text{qt}\) \(1 \text{ ft} = 12 \text{ in}\), \(1 \text{ lb} = 16 \text{ oz}\), \(1 \text{ gal} = 4 \text{ qt}\)
Inter-System Conversions \(\text{mi} \leftrightarrow \text{km}\), \(\text{lb} \leftrightarrow \text{kg}\), \(\text{gal} \leftrightarrow \text{L}\), \(\text{in} \rightarrow \text{cm}\) \(1 \text{ mi} \approx 1.60934 \text{ km}\), \(1 \text{ kg} \approx 2.20462 \text{ lb}\)
\(1 \text{ L} \approx 0.264172 \text{ gal}\), \(1 \text{ in} = 2.54 \text{ cm}\)
