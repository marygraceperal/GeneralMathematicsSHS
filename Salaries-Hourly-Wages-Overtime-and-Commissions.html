💵 Salaries, Hourly Wages, Overtime & Commissions

🎯 Learning Competency

Calculate weekly or monthly wages from an annual salary, wages from an hourly rate, including overtime & allowances.

1. Converting Annual Salary to Pay Period Earnings

A salary is a fixed payment made by an employer to an employee, typically specified as an annual amount regardless of the hours worked. To compute gross earnings per pay cycle, divide the annual salary by the total number of pay periods in a year.

Pay Frequency Periods Per Year ($n$) Conversion Formula
Weekly 52 $\text{Weekly Pay} = \frac{\text{Annual Salary}}{52}$
Bi-weekly (Every 2 weeks) 26 $\text{Bi-weekly Pay} = \frac{\text{Annual Salary}}{26}$
Semi-monthly (Twice a month) 24 $\text{Semi-monthly Pay} = \frac{\text{Annual Salary}}{24}$
Monthly 12 $\text{Monthly Pay} = \frac{\text{Annual Salary}}{12}$

📌 Example 1: Monthly & Weekly Earnings from Salary

Problem: An administrative assistant is hired with an annual salary of $₱360,000$. Calculate their (a) monthly wage and (b) weekly wage.

  1. Monthly Wage: $\frac{₱360,000}{12} = $ ₱30,000.00
  2. Weekly Wage: $\frac{₱360,000}{52} = $ ₱6,923.08
2. Hourly Wages and Overtime Rates

An hourly wage is paid based on the actual hours worked. Standard work schedules typically mandate 8 hours per day or 40 hours per week. Any hours worked beyond standard hours are classified as overtime (OT) and compensated at a higher multiplier rate.

🔑 Overtime Rate Multipliers

Regular OT = 1.25 × Hourly Rate
Rest Day / Special Day OT = 1.30 × Hourly Rate
Regular Holiday OT = 2.00 × Hourly Rate

📌 Example 2: Calculating Wages with Overtime

Problem: Maria earns an hourly rate of $₱120.00$. In a regular week, she worked 40 standard hours plus 6 hours of regular overtime (125% rate).

  1. Regular Pay: $40 \text{ hrs} \times ₱120.00 = ₱4,800.00$
  2. Overtime Hourly Rate: $₱120.00 \times 1.25 = ₱150.00 / \text{hr}$
  3. Overtime Pay: $6 \text{ hrs} \times ₱150.00 = ₱900.00$
  4. Total Weekly Wage: $₱4,800.00 + ₱900.00 = $ ₱5,700.00
3. Allowances & Gross Earnings

Allowances are supplemental monetary benefits provided to cover job-related expenses (e.g., transportation, meal, or communication allowances). Gross wage is the total sum of all earnings before statutory or voluntary deductions.

📐 Total Gross Wage Formula

$\text{Gross Wage} = \text{Basic Pay} + \text{Overtime Pay} + \text{Allowances} + \text{Commissions}$

📌 Example 3: Comprehensive Gross Wage Calculation

Problem: Juan has a monthly basic salary of $₱22,000$. This month, he earned $₱2,400$ in overtime pay, received a $₱1,500$ monthly transportation allowance, and earned $₱3,200$ in sales commission. Calculate his total gross pay for the month.

  1. Basic Salary: $₱22,000.00$
  2. Overtime: $₱2,400.00$
  3. Allowance: $₱1,500.00$
  4. Commission: $₱3,200.00$
  5. Total Gross Wage: $₱22,000 + ₱2,400 + ₱1,500 + ₱3,200 = $ ₱29,100.00
