📐 General Mathematics

Lesson 1.1.A: Salaries, Hourly Wages, Overtime & Commissions

Grade 11 • Term 1 • Week 1

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, you will be able to:

  • Cognitive: Differentiate between fixed salaries, hourly wages, overtime pay, and commissions.
  • Psychomotor: Compute total gross pay accurately given various workplace earning structures.
  • Affective: Evaluate job compensation models to make informed financial choices.

1. Salary vs. Hourly Wage

💼 Fixed Salary

A fixed periodic payment (e.g., monthly or bi-weekly) regardless of hours worked.

Gross = Fixed Rate

⏱️ Hourly Wage

Earnings based on the actual number of hours worked per pay period.

Gross = Hours × Hourly Rate

2. Overtime & Special Rates

Work performed beyond standard daily/weekly hours is compensated at a higher multiplier.

Overtime Pay = Regular Rate × OT Multiplier × OT Hours
Work Scenario Standard Multiplier
Regular Overtime (> 8 hrs/day) 125% (1.25)
Rest Day / Special Holiday 130% (1.30)
Regular Holiday 200% (2.00)

✍️ Worked Example: Overtime

Scenario: Maria earns a regular rate of ₱110.00/hr. She worked 40 regular hours and 6 overtime hours (150% multiplier) this week.

Step 1: Compute Regular Earnings
₱110.00 × 40 hrs = ₱4,400.00

Step 2: Compute Overtime Pay
₱110.00 × 1.5 × 6 hrs = ₱990.00

Step 3: Total Gross Earnings
₱4,400.00 + ₱990.00 = ₱5,390.00

3. Commission Models

🏷️ Straight Commission

Earnings are directly proportional to total sales achieved.

Commission = Total Sales × Commission Rate

📈 Graduated (Tiered) Commission

Commission rates increase as sales hit higher milestone brackets.

✍️ Worked Example: Tiered Commission

Juan earned sales of ₱220,000 using this commission schedule:

  • 3% on the first ₱50,000
  • 5% on sales between ₱50,001 – ₱150,000
  • 8% on sales over ₱150,000

Breakdown:

  • Tier 1: ₱50,000 × 0.03 = ₱1,500
  • Tier 2: ₱100,000 × 0.05 = ₱5,000
  • Tier 3: ₱70,000 × 0.08 = ₱5,600

Total Commission: ₱12,100.00

🧠 Quick Check

An employee worked an 8-hour shift on a regular holiday (200% rate). If their daily base rate is ₱600.00, what is their gross pay for that day?

Answer: ₱1,200.00

(₱600.00 × 200% multiplier)

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Gross pay accounts for base earnings before deductions.
  • Overtime rates depend on the work day classification (regular day vs. rest day vs. holiday).
  • Commission models incentivize higher performance through flat or graduated percentages.
