1. Introduction to Measurement Systems

Unit conversion is a fundamental mathematical skill used across science, engineering, trades, and daily life. Modern measurements are predominantly based on two systems: the Metric System (SI) and the US Customary System. Mastering conversions requires understanding proportional scaling and dimensional analysis.

Key Concept: Dimensional Analysis
Dimensional analysis (the factor-label method) involves multiplying a value by a fraction equal to 1 (a conversion factor) so that undesirable units cancel out, leaving the target unit intact.

2. The Metric System (International System of Units)

The metric system is decimal-based, making conversions straightforward by shifting decimal places using powers of 10.

Example 1: Kilometers to Meters

Problem: Convert 4.5 km into meters.

Step-by-step Solution:
  1. Identify the conversion factor: \(1 \text{ km} = 1,000 \text{ m}\)
  2. Set up dimensional analysis: \(4.5 \text{ km} \times \left( \frac{1000 \text{ m}}{1 \text{ km}} \right)\)
  3. Calculate: \(4.5 \times 1000 = \mathbf{4,500 \text{ m}}\)

3. US Customary System

The US Customary system relies on specific historical scaling factors between units rather than uniform powers of 10.

Common Customary Equivalences

Category Unit Customary Equivalence Metric Equivalent (Approx.)
Length Foot (ft) \(1 \text{ ft} = 12 \text{ in}\) \(1 \text{ ft} \approx 30.48 \text{ cm}\)
Mile (mi) \(1 \text{ mi} = 5,280 \text{ ft}\) \(1 \text{ mi} \approx 1.609 \text{ km}\)
Mass / Weight Pound (lb) \(1 \text{ lb} = 16 \text{ oz}\) \(1 \text{ lb} \approx 0.4536 \text{ kg}\)
Volume Gallon (gal) \(1 \text{ gal} = 4 \text{ qt} = 128 \text{ fl oz}\) \(1 \text{ gal} \approx 3.785 \text{ L}\)

4. Inter-System Conversions (Customary ↔ Metric)

Converting between US Customary and Metric systems requires standard conversion constants.

\[ \text{Target Value} = \text{Given Value} \times \text{Conversion Factor} \]

Key Factors: \(1 \text{ in} = 2.54 \text{ cm}\) | \(1 \text{ mi} \approx 1.609 \text{ km}\) | \(1 \text{ lb} \approx 0.4536 \text{ kg}\) | \(1 \text{ gal} \approx 3.785 \text{ L}\)

Example 2: Miles to Kilometers

Problem: Convert 5 miles into kilometers.

Step-by-step Solution:
  1. Given: Value \(= 5 \text{ mi}\), Factor \(\approx 1.60934 \text{ km/mi}\)
  2. Formula: \(5 \text{ mi} \times 1.60934 \frac{\text{km}}{\text{mi}}\)
  3. Calculation: \(5 \times 1.60934 = \mathbf{8.0467 \text{ km}}\)
