    2. Dimensional Analysis & Equivalence Benchmarks

    Conversions are calculated by multiplying the given metric by a conversion factor equal to 1:

    Given Quantity × ( Target Unit / Given Unit ) = Converted Quantity

    Conversion Equivalence Benchmarks

    • Intra-System (Metric Defaults):
      1 km = 1,000 m  |  1 m = 100 cm = 1,000 mm
      1 kg = 1,000 g  |  1 g = 1,000 mg = 1,000,000 μg
      1 L = 1,000 mL = 1,000 cm3
    • Inter-System (Metric ↔ US Customary):
      Length: 1 inch = 2.54 cm (exact)  |  1 mile ≈ 1.609 km  |  1 meter ≈ 3.281 ft
      Mass / Weight: 1 kg ≈ 2.205 lbs  |  1 lb ≈ 453.6 g
      Volume: 1 US gal ≈ 3.785 L  |  1 fl oz ≈ 29.57 mL

    A. Intra-System Conversions (Metric to Metric)

    1. Kilometers to Meters (3.75 km to m)

    Step 1 (Identify Given & Target): Given = 3.75 km; Target = m.
    Step 2 (Establish Equivalence Factor): 1 km = 1,000 m → Conversion Ratio = ( 1,000 m / 1 km ).
    Step 3 (Set up Dimensional Analysis & Cancel Units):
    3.75 km × ( 1,000 m / 1 km )
    Step 4 (Multiply & Simplify): 3.75 × 1,000 = 3,750 m

    2. Micrograms to Milligrams (8,200 μg to mg)

    Step 1 (Identify Given & Target): Given = 8,200 μg; Target = mg.
    Step 2 (Establish Equivalence Factor): 1 mg = 1,000 μg → Conversion Ratio = ( 1 mg / 1,000 μg ).
    Step 3 (Set up Dimensional Analysis & Cancel Units):
    8,200 μg × ( 1 mg / 1,000 μg )
    Step 4 (Divide & Simplify): 8,200 ÷ 1,000 = 8.2 mg

    B. Inter-System Conversions (Metric ↔ US Customary)

    1. Inches to Centimeters (14 in to cm)

    Step 1 (Identify Given & Target): Given = 14 in; Target = cm.
    Step 2 (Establish Equivalence Factor): 1 in = 2.54 cm → Conversion Ratio = ( 2.54 cm / 1 in ).
    Step 3 (Set up Dimensional Analysis & Cancel Units):
    14 in × ( 2.54 cm / 1 in )
    Step 4 (Multiply & Simplify): 14 × 2.54 = 35.56 cm

    2. Gallons to Liters (15 US gal to L)

    Step 1 (Identify Given & Target): Given = 15 gal; Target = L.
    Step 2 (Establish Equivalence Factor): 1 gal ≈ 3.785 L → Conversion Ratio = ( 3.785 L / 1 gal ).
    Step 3 (Set up Dimensional Analysis & Cancel Units):
    15 gal × ( 3.785 L / 1 gal )
    Step 4 (Multiply & Simplify): 15 × 3.785 = 56.775 L

    4. Scientific Notation

    Scientific notation (a × 10n, where 1 ≤ |a| < 10) simplifies extreme magnitudes:

    • Microscopic: Diameter of a human red blood cell = 0.000007 m = 7.0 × 10-6 m (7 μm).
    • Astronomical: Mean distance from Earth to the Sun = 149,600,000 km = 1.496 × 108 km.
