1. Fundamental Concepts

Annuity: A sequence of equal periodic payments made at equal intervals of time (e.g., monthly deposits, quarterly loan repayments).
Time Value of Money

Money available at the present time is worth more than the same amount in the future due to its potential earning capacity (interest).

Key Variables
  • \(R\) = Periodic Payment Amount
  • \(r\) = Annual Interest Rate
  • \(m\) = Compounding Frequency
  • \(i = \frac{r}{m}\) = Rate per period
  • \(n = m \times t\) = Total periods

2. Future Value of Ordinary Annuity (\(F\))

Calculates total accumulated savings or investments over time with interest.

\[ F = R \left[ \frac{(1 + i)^n - 1}{i} \right] \]

Practice: Future Value (\(F\))

Problem 1 (Monthly Savings): Maria deposits ₱2,000 at the end of every month for 3 years at 6% compounded monthly.
\(R=2,000, i=\frac{0.06}{12}=0.005, n=36\)
Answer: ₱78,672.22
Problem 2 (Quarterly Fund): An investor places ₱15,000 every quarter into a fund earning 8% compounded quarterly for 5 years.
\(R=15,000, i=\frac{0.08}{4}=0.02, n=20\)
Answer: ₱364,460.58
Problem 3 (Semi-Annual Account): A business sets aside ₱50,000 every 6 months for 4 years at 5% compounded semi-annually.
\(R=50,000, i=\frac{0.05}{2}=0.025, n=8\)
Answer: ₱436,805.35

3. Present Value of Ordinary Annuity (\(P\))

Calculates current principal amount (or initial loan balance) supported by regular payments.

\[ P = R \left[ \frac{1 - (1 + i)^{-n}}{i} \right] \]

Practice: Present Value (\(P\))

Problem 1 (Car Loan Value): Juan pays ₱12,000 monthly for 2 years at 9% interest compounded monthly. What is the current loan value?
\(R=12,000, i=\frac{0.09}{12}=0.0075, n=24\)
Answer: ₱262,669.70
Problem 2 (Equipment Financing): A bakery makes quarterly installments of ₱18,000 for 3 years at 10% compounded quarterly. What is the present cost?
\(R=18,000, i=\frac{0.10}{4}=0.025, n=12\)
Answer: ₱184,639.77
Problem 3 (Annual Pension): A contract pays ₱100,000 at the end of each year for 6 years at 4% compounded annually. What is its present worth?
\(R=100,000, i=0.04, n=6\)
Answer: ₱524,213.71

4. Required Periodic Payment (\(R\))

Determines periodic payment required to fully amortize/pay off a given loan amount (\(P\)).

\[ R = \frac{P \cdot i}{1 - (1 + i)^{-n}} \]

Required Payment: ₱8,219.01

Practice: Amortization / Payment (\(R\))

Problem 1 (Personal Loan): A teacher borrows ₱150,000 at 6% annual interest compounded monthly over 3 years. Find the monthly payment.
\(P=150,000, i=\frac{0.06}{12}=0.005, n=36\)
Answer: ₱4,568.42 / month
Problem 2 (Housing Loan): A family secures a ₱500,000 loan at 8% compounded quarterly for 5 years. What is the required quarterly payment?
\(P=500,000, i=\frac{0.08}{4}=0.02, n=20\)
Answer: ₱30,578.36 / quarter
Problem 3 (Business Appliance): Equipment worth ₱80,000 is financed at 12% compounded monthly for 1 year. Calculate the monthly installment.
\(P=80,000, i=\frac{0.12}{12}=0.01, n=12\)
Answer: ₱7,107.98 / month

5. Spreadsheet Integration (Excel / Google Sheets)

Teach students how to solve annuity problems in modern digital spreadsheets:

Future Value Function:
=FV(rate_per_period, nper, -pmt)
Example (Deposit ₱3,500/mo at 5% for 4 yrs): =FV(0.05/12, 48, -3500)
Present Value Function:
=PV(rate_per_period, nper, -pmt)
Example (Pay ₱8,000/qtr at 10% for 3 yrs): =PV(0.10/4, 12, -8000)
Payment Amortization Function:
=PMT(rate_per_period, nper, -pv)
Example (Borrow ₱350,000 at 6% for 4 yrs): =PMT(0.06/12, 48, -350000)

6. Lesson Summary

  • Annuities represent real-world repetitive cash flows in loans, insurance, and investments.
  • Use Future Value (\(F\)) when projecting compound growth of regular deposits over time.
  • Use Present Value (\(P\)) when determining the current value or initial borrowable principal of equal loan installments.
  • Spreadsheet tools like Excel/Google Sheets mirror mathematical formulas via built-in functions (FV, PV, PMT).
