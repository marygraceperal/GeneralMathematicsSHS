Activity Sheet 1.1.A: Earning Money — Wages, Overtime & Commissions
💡 Key Concept Reference:
  • Gross Pay: Total earnings before any payroll deductions.
  • Overtime Pay: Regular Hourly Rate × Overtime Multiplier × Overtime Hours
  • Straight Commission: Total Sales × Commission Rate
  • Graduated Commission: Sum of commissions earned across tiered sales brackets.

Part I: Computational Skills

  • 1. Alex: ₱3,931.25 (Regular: 40 × ₱85 = ₱3,400 | OT: 5 × [₱85 × 1.25] = ₱531.25)
  • 2. Bea: ₱24,000.00 (Base: ₱18,000 + Commission: ₱120,000 × 0.05 = ₱6,000)
  • 3. Carlo: ₱4,200.00 (Regular 5 Days: 5 × ₱600 = ₱3,000 | Regular Holiday: ₱600 × 200% = ₱1,200)

Part II: Applied Word Problems

  • 1. Maria: Regular Pay = 40 × ₱110 = ₱4,400. OT Pay = 6 × (₱110 × 1.5) = ₱990. Total Gross Pay = ₱5,390.00.
  • 2. Juan:
    Tier 1: ₱50,000 × 0.03 = ₱1,500
    Tier 2: ₱100,000 × 0.05 = ₱5,000
    Tier 3: ₱70,000 × 0.08 = ₱5,600
    Total Commission = ₱12,100.00.
  • 3. Compensation Options:
    Option A: ₱22,000 + (₱250,000 × 0.02) = ₱27,000.00
    Option B: ₱250,000 × 0.10 = ₱25,000.00
    Conclusion: Option A yields higher earnings by ₱2,000.00.

Part III: Real-World Financial Analysis

  • a. Break-Even Point: Daily Hourly Pay = ₱75 × 8 = ₱600. Required Units = ₱600 / ₱45 = 13.33 units (or 14 units to meet/exceed).
  • b. Recommendation:
    Piece-rate Earnings (16 units) = 16 × ₱45 = ₱720.00/day.
    Hourly Earnings = ₱600.00/day.
    Choice: Piece-rate System, as it yields an extra ₱120.00 per day compared to the fixed hourly rate.
