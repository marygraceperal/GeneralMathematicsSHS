General Mathematics (Grade 11)

Interactive Three-Term Budget of Work with Activity Links

Content Standard

The learners should have knowledge and understanding of:

  • Business and finance through problems involving earning money, buying, and selling
  • Patterns, arithmetic and geometric sequences and series
  • Financial application of sequence and series
  • Measurement and conversion
  • Functions and its graphs

Performance Standard

By the end of the term, the learners are able to:

  • Prepare accurate payrolls and digital payslips with correct gross pay, deductions, and net income.
  • Create and present a digital financial report with graphs, equations, and justified real-life decisions.
  • Design and present a practical measurement or conversion solution with a concrete or visual model.

Content Standard

The learners should have knowledge and understanding of piecewise functions, statistical variables, basic trigonometry, and random variables.

Performance Standard

By the end of the term, learners model real-world problems with piecewise functions, analyze data with technology, and construct 3D geometric objects.

  • Solve practical problems involving angles of elevation/depression and navigation bearings. 📄 Activity Sheet

  • Estimate distances, perimeter, and area of residential spaces (renovations/layouts). 📄 Activity Sheet

Content Standard

Knowledge of compound interest, simple/general annuities, loans, hypothesis testing, linear regression, and logical propositions.

Performance Standard

Develop digital financial proposals, conduct data investigations with statistical tests, and evaluate logical propositions and truth values.

Week Domain / Unit Learning Competencies & Subtopics
1 Number and Algebra
  • Solve problems involving compound interest, maturity value, nominal rates, and number of terms. 📄 Activity Sheet
2 Number and Algebra
  • Illustrate simple, general, and deferred annuities and solve corresponding problems. 📄 Activity Sheet
3 Number and Algebra
  • Calculate the fair market value of a cash flow stream that includes an annuity. 📄 Activity Sheet
4 Number and Algebra
  • Differentiate business and consumer loans and solve amortization/mortgage problems. 📄 Activity Sheet
5 Data and Probability
  • Formulate null/alternative hypotheses, significance levels, and identify Type I/II errors. 📄 Activity Sheet
6 Data and Probability
  • Test hypotheses for population means using z-test, one-sample t-test, and paired/independent t-tests. 📄 Activity Sheet
7 Data and Probability
  • Construct scatter plots, line of best fit, and calculate Pearson's r correlation. 📄 Activity Sheet
8 Number and Algebra
  • Identify logical propositions and differentiate simple from compound propositions. 📄 Activity Sheet
9 Number and Algebra
  • Illustrate basic logical operations and conditional proposition forms. 📄 Activity Sheet
10 Number and Algebra
  • Illustrate tautologies, contradictions, and determine truth values in practical situations. 📄 Activity Sheet
