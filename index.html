📐 General Mathematics (Grade 11)

Interactive Three-Term Budget of Work with Alphanumeric Reference Codes

🧠 Content Standard (Term 1)

The learners should have knowledge and understanding of:

  • 1.1 Business and finance through problems involving earning money, buying, and selling
  • 1.2 Patterns, arithmetic and geometric sequences and series
  • 1.3 Financial application of sequence and series
  • 1.4 Measurement and conversion
  • 1.5 Functions and its graphs

🎯 Performance Standard (Term 1)

By the end of the term, the learners are able to:

  • P1.1 Prepare accurate payrolls and digital payslips with correct gross pay, deductions, and net income.
  • P1.2 Create and present a digital financial report with graphs, equations, and justified real-life decisions.
  • P1.3 Design and present a practical measurement or conversion solution with a concrete or visual model.
